A Day in an Animal's Life

How is honey made? How does a mole live underground? And why can't you hear an owl coming?

In "A Day in an Animal's Life" we follow one animal through a whole day in every episode: from waking up to going to sleep. Along the way we answer a question that children really ask.

  • 🐝 For children aged 5 to 8
  • ⏱️ Each episode is about three minutes long
  • ✅ Every fact is checked – nothing is made up
  • 🎬 Lovingly animated, calmly told

For watching together, wondering and asking more questions.
